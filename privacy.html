Privacy

A public guide.
Not a data collection service.

This website helps you understand and set up AI-in-One. It does not connect to your Microsoft 365 tenant or process your report data.

What this site does

Pages, screenshots and download links are publicly hosted. Screenshots use fictional data. Search runs in your browser. We do not request file uploads, tenant credentials or Microsoft 365 sign-in.

Browser storage

The site can remember your light or dark theme on your device. No analytics, session replay, advertising trackers or tracking cookies are added by this site. Do not put credentials, tenant data or private file paths in URLs.

Hosting, downloads and videos

GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary request metadata under its policies. Downloads come from GitHub-hosted project files. External sites have their own privacy policies.

The YouTube setup video is not loaded until you select Play; it then uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed. The report overview video is hosted by GitHub and loads when played. Video providers may process network information when you play their content.

Your report and source data

Data collection and reporting happen in your environment using PAX, Fabric OneLake and Power BI. They have their own permissions, logs and storage. Microsoft does not receive access to the data you load simply because you use this template.

Minimum Group Size controls display detail; it does not anonymize the data. Report security roles do not protect direct access to source files or tables.

Responsible use

Your organization is responsible for lawful collection, employee-reporting policies, privacy, access, retention, export and sharing. Audit telemetry should not be the only source used for licensing decisions or complete usage reporting. Compare it with the Microsoft 365 admin center and Viva Insights where appropriate.

The report does not measure employee performance, actual time saved, response quality or business outcomes. AI-in-One is currently available in English.
